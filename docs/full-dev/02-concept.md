# Phase 2: World Concepts (two-object method)

_Status: **draft**, awaiting your pick (2026-11-20)._

## How these were made
Every world comes from **one identity object (A)** and **one unexpected object (B)**, following the full-dev method: five forced connections first, then the natural connections that really exist. The objects are the foundation of the world, not illustrations inside it.

**Identity objects (A).** Each one comes only from your brief.
| A | Why (brief) |
|---|---|
| **The Move** | "Every problem has a better move." |
| **The Thread** | One unbroken line, from idea to shipped product, and from Lahore to the world. |
| **The Prototype** | "A designer and engineer in one person", the thing that exists between an idea and a product. |

**Unexpected objects (B) I considered:** origami sheet · Jacquard loom · river · marble run · camera obscura · tangram · seedling · kite · rock-salt crystal · mechanical watch · letterpress.

**Rejected:**
- **Kite:** Basant is a Lahore cliché, and kite string is tied to injuries in public memory.
- **Rock-salt crystal:** crystalline shards are too close to igloo.inc.
- **Mechanical watch:** a luxury cliché with heavy geometry.
- **Letterpress:** it pulls toward the "broadsheet" AI-design tell.

**Content each world has to hold** (from the approved Phase 1): hero (name, one-liner, "Start a project") · philosophy · 6 genres · what you deliver · toolkit · about/education · projects "in the workshop" · contact. The signature tool is not included.

## 1 · CREASE: "One sheet, every form"
**A** The Move × **B** a folded sheet of paper (origami)

**Forced connections**
1. A fold is a move: it changes everything after it.
2. The same sheet becomes any form, like one person building six genres.
3. The crease pattern is the blueprint (engineer). The folded form is the design (designer).
4. Flat to 3D is idea to product.
5. A better fold needs fewer creases, which mirrors lightweight builds.

**Natural connections:** crease patterns can be computed, and computational origami is a real field. Folding gives thin material stiffness; it is how the Miura fold packs deployable solar arrays. Folds are only visible through light and shadow.

| Content | Place / state in the world |
|---|---|
| Hero | A single flat sheet floating in soft light, with the name printed on it |
| Philosophy | The first fold: one crease divides the sheet |
| 6 genres | The sheet refolds into six distinct abstract forms, and the camera orbits each |
| What you deliver | A macro flight along a corrugated surface, with each strength as a ridge |
| Toolkit (T4) | The sheet unfolds flat to show its full crease pattern |
| Projects (workshop) | Sheets with creases scored but not yet folded |
| Contact | A fresh blank sheet comes to the camera: "bring the idea" |

- **Hero moment:** the cursor drags a grazing light across the sheet. The hidden crease pattern only appears under that light, and the first scroll pulls the first fold.
- **Material & light:** matte fibrous paper, one soft key light, crisp contact shadows.
- **Palette:** Sheet `#E3E5E0` · Shade `#A9AEA9` · Graphite `#22262A` · Valley `#2C4FD4` · Mountain `#E04A2F`. Crease diagrams often draw valley and mountain folds in two colours, which is where the accents come from.
- **Loader:** the crease pattern scores itself line by line, and every loaded asset scores its creases. That is the real progress.
- **Budget phone:** the lightest of the seven: one deforming plane with baked shadows.
- **Risk:** it must never show cranes or animal folds.

## 2 · WARP: "Where design and code were the same machine"
**A** The Thread × **B** the Jacquard loom

**Forced connections**
1. Every pass of the shuttle is a move that decides the pattern.
2. Warp (fixed structure, engineering) × weft (colour, design) makes one cloth.
3. Punched cards are code that becomes pattern.
4. Thread → cloth → garment follows idea → product → shipped.
5. Cloth travels: textiles are Pakistan's largest export industry, which carries Lahore to the world.

**Natural connections:** the Jacquard loom's punched cards inspired Babbage's Analytical Engine. Ada Lovelace wrote that it "weaves algebraical patterns just as the Jacquard-loom weaves flowers and leaves", so design and computing share an ancestor. Threads only hold a pattern under tension, which means structure is what makes the beauty possible.

| Content | Place / state |
|---|---|
| Hero | Low along thousands of taut warp threads running into depth, with the name woven in a band |
| Philosophy | One punched card: changing one hole changes the whole pattern |
| 6 genres | Six woven bands, each a different weave structure |
| What you deliver | The camera passes through the shed as threads lift and part to reveal each strength |
| Toolkit (T4) | A wall of spools, one per tool |
| Projects (workshop) | Cloth still on the loom, with an unfinished edge and loose threads |
| Contact | A single loose thread reaches toward you: pull it to start |

- **Hero moment:** on the first scroll, half the threads lift in unison, the shuttle passes, and the one-liner is woven in.
- **Material & light:** cotton and silk with directional sheen, warm raking daylight from a high window, lint floating in the beam.
- **Palette:** Cotton `#E7E2D6` · Indigo `#1B2A55` · Madder `#A3322C` · Loom oak `#5B4332` · Brass `#B58A3E`. Indigo and madder are the two dyes of Sindhi ajrak, so the region is felt through the dye, not stamped on as a pattern.
- **Loader:** warp threads are strung onto the loom one by one, and the thread count follows the real bytes loaded.
- **Budget phone:** medium. Thread count scales by device tier. The woven text needs a custom shader, which is the showpiece and also more build time.
- **Risk:** indigo can drift toward "dark mode + purple", so the room must stay in daylight.

## 3 · DELTA: "From the source to the open sea"
**A** The Thread × **B** a river

**Forced connections**
1. Water always finds the better path.
2. The source is the idea; the sea is the shipped product.
3. Tributaries joining are disciplines merging in one person.
4. The river carves terrain (engineering) and its meanders draw patterns (design).
5. The river ends where ships leave, which is shipped and global.

**Natural connections:** the Indus runs the length of Pakistan into the Arabian Sea. Terrain contours are layered slices, which makes them parallax depth in literal form. A delta is never finished; it is always still forming.

| Content | Place / state |
|---|---|
| Hero | One drop falls from a high source into a still basin, and the ripples carry the name |
| Philosophy | The water meets a rock and takes the better path around it |
| 6 genres | Six tributaries join, each from its own valley |
| What you deliver | Plains where each strength is a terrace the water reaches |
| Toolkit (T4) | Riverbed stones, one per tool |
| Projects (workshop) | Sandbars of the delta, still forming |
| Contact | The coastline at dawn with a harbour light: "set sail" |

- **Hero moment:** drop, then ripple, then the camera tips forward and starts flying downstream on scroll.
- **Material & light:** stacked contour layers like cut sediment, a low golden sun, a simple reflective water shader.
- **Palette:** Silt `#CDBB9C` · Riverbed `#3F3327` · River jade `#2D7A70` · Haze `#DDE4E1` · Harbour light `#F2A93B`.
- **Loader:** water fills the contour basin, and the water level is the real progress.
- **Budget phone:** very light. It is the best fit for "layered depth/parallax".
- **Risk:** it could read as a travel site, so no map shapes or country outline.

## 4 · THE RUN: "Release the idea. Watch it ship."
**A** The Prototype × **B** a marble run (gravity machine)

**Forced connections**
1. Every switch on the track is a move.
2. One ball carries one idea through the whole machine, end to end.
3. The machine is built to be watched (design) and to work every time (engineering).
4. Release → landing in the cup is idea → shipped.
5. A better track loses less energy, which is performance.

**Natural connections:** a gravity machine is pure cause and effect, like code running. Rails depend on precise tolerances. Every mechanism makes a sound, which is the hook for sound design.

| Content | Place / state |
|---|---|
| Hero | A chrome ball held at the top of a precise machine. "Start a project" is the release |
| Philosophy | A switch flips the ball onto a shorter track |
| 6 genres | A rotating selector sends the ball down six tracks in turn |
| What you deliver | Mechanisms (lift, spiral, bell, gate), one per strength |
| Toolkit (T4) | The parts tray |
| Projects (workshop) | An exposed, unfinished track section with tools beside it |
| Contact | The ball drops into the cup with a click |

- **Hero moment:** the first scroll releases the ball, and the camera chases it down the first drop.
- **Material & light:** brushed aluminium rails, maple blocks, chrome reflections, studio softbox light, a cast-concrete wall.
- **Palette:** Concrete `#9C9D98` · Aluminium `#C3C7CA` · Maple `#C99A62` · Ink `#1F2226` · Signal orange `#FF5B1F`.
- **Loader:** a screw lift raises the ball, and the lift height is the real progress. At the top, the ball waits for the visitor.
- **Budget phone:** medium. Procedural tubes, with physics faked along curves.
- **Risk:** it looks like a toy if the materials aren't precise. Sound is the payoff but must stay off by default.

## 5 · OBSCURA: "Bring the idea into focus"
**A** The Prototype × **B** the camera obscura

**Forced connections**
1. Refocusing is a move: the same scene, a better view.
2. Blur → sharp is idea → product.
3. Optics (engineering) produces an image (design).
4. The whole outside world projected into one small dark room is Lahore to the world.
5. A pinhole needs less, not more, which mirrors lightweight builds.

**Natural connections:** Ibn al-Haytham gave the first clear explanation of the camera obscura in his *Book of Optics*. Focus is a natural hierarchy, because what is sharp is what matters, which maps directly onto your T1–T5 tiers. Light needs darkness to be seen.

| Content | Place / state |
|---|---|
| Hero | A dark room where a beam through a pinhole projects the world upside-down, with the name in the beam |
| Philosophy | The lens turns and the image flips upright |
| 6 genres | Six projections, cast one after another on different walls |
| What you deliver | A rack focus pulls each strength sharp |
| Toolkit (T4) | A drawer of lenses |
| Projects (workshop) | Undeveloped plates |
| Contact | The shutter: "take the shot" |

- **Hero moment:** the first scroll flips the projected world upright and snaps it into focus.
- **Material & light:** a warm dark room, dust in the beam, frosted glass.
- **Palette:** Room `#1F1A15` · Beam `#F4E8CC` · Dust gold `#C79E57` · Blue hour `#6C8DA4` · Glass `#A9B3B0`.
- **Loader:** the aperture widens as assets load, and brightness is the real progress.
- **Budget phone:** real depth-of-field is expensive, so it is faked with pre-blurred layers.
- **Risk:** this is the darkest concept and must not drift into generic dark mode.

## 6 · SEVEN PIECES: "Same pieces. Better move."
**A** The Move × **B** the tangram

**Forced connections**
1. A tangram is solved by moves, not by adding pieces.
2. The same seven pieces make any figure: one skill set, many products.
3. Geometry (engineering) makes figures (design).
4. The square is the idea; the figure is the product.
5. No piece is wasted, which is lightweight.

**Natural connections:** every figure folds back into the perfect square, so every solution can be reversed. Constraint breeds creativity. A figure reads from its silhouette alone, which is clarity.

| Content | Place / state |
|---|---|
| Hero | Seven honed-stone pieces resting as a perfect square, with the name engraved across them |
| Philosophy | One piece rotates and the square opens |
| 6 genres | The pieces rearrange into six abstract figures |
| What you deliver | The pieces stack into a structure for each strength |
| Toolkit (T4) | The pieces laid out with labels |
| Projects (workshop) | An unsolved silhouette, waiting |
| Contact | An empty silhouette that the visitor's idea will fill |

- **Hero moment:** a piece lifts slightly as the cursor nears it, and the first scroll breaks the square.
- **Material & light:** honed stone with sharp chamfers, a single museum spotlight, deep soft shadows.
- **Palette:** Basalt `#2D3032` · Sandstone `#C8A47C` · Jade stone `#6F8D7C` · Lapis `#2C4A8C` · Plinth `#D6D2CB`.
- **Loader:** the pieces slide into the square one at a time: seven real loading stages.
- **Budget phone:** the lightest geometry of all, seven meshes.
- **Risk:** it can feel like a puzzle game, and the figures can slide into literal icons.

## 7 · PHOTOTROPISM: "Design above. Engineering below."
**A** The Prototype × **B** a seedling growing toward light

**Forced connections**
1. A plant bends toward the better light, which is the better move.
2. Seed → plant is idea → product.
3. Above the soil is design (visible); below it is engineering (roots). One organism.
4. Seeds travel on the wind, Lahore to the world.
5. Growth follows rules, and L-systems are how computers grow plants, so growth is code.

**Natural connections:** plants grow toward light (phototropism). Branching follows mathematical rules. The root system is the hidden half of the plant.

| Content | Place / state |
|---|---|
| Hero | A seed in dark soil under a single light, with the name |
| Philosophy | The shoot bends toward the light |
| 6 genres | Six branches |
| What you deliver | Design and motion are the leaves above; the camera dips below the soil, where performance, security and reliability are the roots |
| Toolkit (T4) | A map of the roots |
| Projects (workshop) | Buds that haven't opened yet |
| Contact | Seeds released into the wind |

- **Hero moment:** on desktop the cursor is the light and the sprout bends toward it. On mobile, touch does the same.
- **Material & light:** damp soil, backlit translucent leaves, greenhouse haze.
- **Palette:** Soil `#241C16` · Celadon `#9EB9A1` · Grow amber `#F1B45E` · Glass haze `#DADFD8` · Root ivory `#E6DCC8`.
- **Loader:** roots grow underground, and root length is the real progress.
- **Budget phone:** medium. The growth is generated once, procedurally.
- **Risk:** "growth" is a common marketing metaphor. The above/below split is what makes it yours.

## My recommendation
**#1 pick: 2 · WARP.** It is the only concept where "designer + engineer in one" is a historical fact rather than a metaphor: the loom is where pattern-making and computing began. Its Pakistan link is real (textiles) and felt through material, not symbols. And "in the workshop" is natural: cloth still on the loom.
**Runner-up: 1 · CREASE.** The lightest on budget phones and the cleanest single idea.
