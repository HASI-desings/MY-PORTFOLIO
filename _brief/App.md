## 1. What this is

**HASI DESIGNS** is the personal digital identity of **Hussnain Sohail** — software engineering student, developer and digital creator from Lahore, Pakistan.

It is **not** a conventional developer portfolio. It is a **cinematic, scroll-controlled digital film** made of six connected scenes. The visitor moves through it with scroll, hover and tap. **Everything is live code** — no pre-rendered video, no video scrubbing, no screenshots of animations. Every element reacts to the visitor.

The only photographic asset of the creator is **one image: `ME.png`** — a rim-lit silhouette portrait — and it appears **only once, in the final scene**. The whole site builds toward that reveal.

The visitor should leave remembering **the experience, the work, and the person behind it**.

## 3. The six scenes (full spec in Design.md)

### SCENE 01 — THE IDENTITY
A dark, nearly empty obsidian space. Enormous **HASI** lettering is revealed as a physical, architectural structure standing in the room — lit, with real depth and a thin red rim of light. A sharp red diagonal plane slices the composition. Then quietly: **HUSSNAIN SOHAIL** · *Founder / Developer / Digital Creator* · the one-line statement. **No person appears.** The lettering is the hero.

### SCENE 02 — THE TOOLKIT
The space becomes a three-dimensional workspace. At its center floats **the Core** — a slowly turning matte-obsidian monolith with red edge light, an abstract stand-in for the creator. Around it, the tools he really uses float at different depths, enter one by one, drift and rotate gently. Hover/tap a tool → it lifts toward the viewer and reveals its name and one honest line.

### SCENE 03 — WHAT I CREATE
Six disciplines, each a chapter with its own code-built visual world (never six identical cards):
01 Websites & Digital Experiences · 02 AI Automation & Agentic Systems · 03 Custom Software & SaaS · 04 POS & Business Management · 05 Trading & Financial Technology · 06 UI/UX & Product Design.
The visitor understands what he builds without reading paragraphs.

### SCENE 04 — SELECTED PROJECTS
Real work as large image plates resting on a dark surface. Hover → the plate lifts off the surface with depth and shadow, and **OPEN PROJECT** appears. Honest status: **LIVE · IN DEVELOPMENT · CONCEPT**. Live projects open their real URL in a new tab; others open an internal detail view. Data-driven for future projects.

### SCENE 05 — THE PHILOSOPHY
Pure typography and light. Enormous ice-grey **CREATIVITY IS A PROCESS, NOT A TRAIT.** assembles word by word with scroll, lit by a moving light that rakes across the letters. Three supporting lines appear one at a time. The red diagonal returns to close the scene.

### SCENE 06 — THE FINAL INTERACTION (the reveal)
For the first time, the creator appears: `ME.png`, his silhouette portrait, rises out of the obsidian with a red rim of light. Behind him, huge type: **EVERY IDEA STARTS SOMEWHERE.** — then **LET'S BUILD SOMETHING.** A single tactile red button, **LET'S MAKE IT REAL**, rises from the bottom of the screen to the lower-middle, over the lower part of the portrait. Clicking reveals **EMAIL** and **WHATSAPP**, both prefilled. Instagram sits quietly in the corner.

## 4. Why it is built this way (keep this reasoning)

- **The person is held back until the end.** Brand → capability → work → thinking → *then* the human. The reveal is the payoff, and the CTA sits on it.
- **The Core in Scene 02** carries the creator's presence without showing him — it is the same matte obsidian + red rim language his portrait uses in Scene 06, so the reveal feels connected.
- **Red is a signal, never a surface.** It marks actions, active states and transitions.
- **One idea per screen.** Information is layered (Design.md → Visibility Hierarchy) so it never feels packed.
- **Live code everywhere**, so every element responds to the visitor.

## 6. Success criteria

1. Feels like an art-directed interactive film within 5 seconds.
2. Strictly the HASI palette.
3. Oversized typography is the main visual language.
4. Real projects, working links, nothing fabricated.
5. Purposeful motion — never everything moving at once.
6. Works and looks intentional on **every** screen size and orientation (Design.md §13).
7. Loads efficiently; never leaves the user on a broken or frozen screen (Security.md).
8. No generic AI/template aesthetic.
9. All personal content editable from one content folder.
10. Usability beats spectacle when they conflict.
